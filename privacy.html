Privacy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Who runs this website

This is the personal website of Nick Coffyn. Contact: nickcoffyn@me.com.

Visitor statistics

I count page visits with Matomo, which I run myself on the same server that hosts this website. It is used only to see how many people visit which pages.

  • It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • Sent for each visit: the page address without query details, the page title, the time, and your browser’s standard information (browser and device type, language).
  • Your IP address is shortened (the last three parts are removed) before it is stored. Only the shortened address is used to estimate the country.
  • Referring pages are not stored. Visits are not linked across days or to other websites.
  • Raw visit records are deleted after 30 days. Aggregated statistics are kept for 12 months.
  • The data is not shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, no statistics are collected.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Files loaded from cdnjs

The home page loads its icons (Font Awesome) and background animation (particles.js) from cdnjs, a public file service run by Cloudflare. Your browser connects to cdnjs directly to fetch these files, so Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser information under its own privacy policy. This privacy page does not load them.

Server logs

The web server keeps a technical log of requests (IP address, time, page requested and browser type) to troubleshoot problems and detect abuse. The log is size-limited and overwritten automatically.

Email and links

If you email me, I use your message and address only to reply. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and Folding@home take you to those services, which apply their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, or object to it, by writing to nickcoffyn@me.com. Because IP addresses are shortened, I may not be able to identify which statistics relate to you.

You can also complain to the Luxembourg data protection authority: Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), 15, Boulevard du Jazz, L-4370 Belvaux, cnpd.public.lu.
