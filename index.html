About

systems.builder / ai.operator / operational.reality

I have spent most of my life around computers, systems and technology. I started experimenting with computers around age eight and built most of my technical understanding through reading, tinkering, breaking things, fixing things, and applying logic in the real world.

Professionally, I have worked across customer service, quality, operations, product, AI transformation and process design in Amazon, Mercedes-Benz and Google/Nest environments. My real strength is connecting operational reality with technical possibility: understanding how people, tools, data and incentives interact, then building systems that make work clearer, faster and more useful.

I am interested in the places where technology meets messy reality: AI, automation, hardware, robotics, recycling, data systems, market intelligence, knowledge systems and operational transformation.
